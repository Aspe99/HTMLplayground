Music

I am a rather controversial person when it comes to music. I really like music from the romantic period, and being a pianist, I usually play music from the romantic period. Two of my favourite composers are:

  • Chopin
  • Rachmaninoff

      • I find that Chopin is really good at taking major keys, and making them sound sad. Chopin did suffer from depression. His works are very relaxing, and maeningful. Rachmaninoff was also a romantic composer, and this is very obvious if you listen to something like his Piano Concerto no.2, specifically the second movement.     • Fantaisie Impromputu. This is considered the 'original' work that Chopin wrote, despite it being less popular. It was Arthur Rubenstein that discovered the score, when he bough it at an auction. I think that it is only fair that I then let you listen to him playing it. I do prefer this version slightly.

    Pianists

    I can be rather selective about who is performing. If the piece is slightly too fast, I turn it off and listen to sommething else. I am very picky. For example, I like Lang Lang playing 'La Campanella', as he is a very technically advanced pianist. There are some other recordings out there that I do like, as they emphasise certain parts more. Lang Lang plays the Coda very well, whereas other performers seem to like being quiet and playing stacato. On the other hand, I really don't like Lang Lang playing Fantasie Impromputu, as he shows off and plays it too quickly. Someone like 'German Kitkin' plays it much better. IMSLP is one of the best places to go to see different performers and listen to vastly different recordings. I find that the most popular person in terms of my saved recording on IMSLP is 'Luis Kolodin', which is apparently a stage name for 'Fellipe Sarro'.
